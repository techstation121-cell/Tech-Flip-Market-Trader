# 🕹️ Tech-Flip-Market-Trader

A fast-paced market simulation game built in HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. Buy vintage tech, diagnose hardware issues, repair devices on your workbench, and flip items for massive profit while navigating live market news trends!

---

## 🔥 Features

- 📈 **Dynamic Economy & Market Events:** Prices swing daily based on news trends (Retro Craze, RAM Shortages, GPU Crashes).
- 🛒 **Flea Market & Auctions:** Hunt for bargain items in various conditions (`Broken`, `Dusty`, `Working`, `Mint`).
- 🛠️ **Interactive Workbench:** Clean pins, replace blown capacitors, upgrade memory, and apply thermal paste to maximize profit margins.
- 🏪 **Store Management:** Manage your shelf capacity, set markup prices, sell to walk-in customers, or quick-sell for fast cash.
- 🎨 **Retro Cyberpunk UI:** Lightweight, responsive design with dark mode retro aesthetic and synth audio effects.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Browse Listings:** Check the **Flea Market** daily for cheap or broken hardware deals.
2. **Repair & Upgrade:** Take items to your **Workbench** to fix flaws and increase their market value.
3. **Watch the News:** Read daily news alerts—sell high during market demand spikes!
4. **Expand Your Store:** Accumulate cash and build your store reputation to become the ultimate retro tech mogul.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5 & CSS3** (Custom retro dark UI styling)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Game loop, dynamic pricing logic, inventory state)
- **Web Audio API** (Sound effects and audio cues)
